Dial sections

Each section is one piece of the line, and the sections join end to start in order. Pick a type for each: a Bézier curve with handles, a Sine piece, a Flat stretch or an instant Jump. Values are recorded as you go and kept in this browser.

Drag any dot to edit it. Hollow dots are the Bézier handles and dark dots mark where sections join. The first point is where the wave starts and only moves up and down. The square at the end sits at the same height as the first point: the dark line up or down to it is where the wave jumps back to the start to repeat. The thin line running across is the middle line, and the dashed lines mark the limit of 1 above and below it.

Play

Plays the line as one repeating cycle. Changing the frequency changes the pitch, even while it plays.

Hold a key to play a note for as long as you hold it: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; are piano keys starting from C4, and Z and X move down and up an octave.

First point

Where the wave starts. It only moves up and down.

Scale to fit stretches the wave up or down and centres it, so it fills the band from −1 to 1 without changing its shape.

In your own code, call getDialValues() to get this same data. A Sine section is part of a sine wave from its start height to its end height: “Both ends” is half a cycle, smooth at both ends, and “End only” or “Start only” is a quarter cycle that is smooth at that end. Two “Both ends” sections, low to high and back, make one exact sine cycle. A Flat section stays at its start height, and a Jump has no length. Length can be any number from 0 up. Length and handle x are measured from the section’s start. The handles are kept where the curve can never go backwards: Handle 1 x can reach up to 4/3 of the length and Handle 2 x down to −1/3 of it, but how far each one can go depends on where the other is. If a shorter length leaves the handles no room, they move in towards the start just enough to fit. Every y value is the height above or below the middle line. First point and End y go from −1 to 1. The handles can go further, as far as the curve itself stays between −1 and 1; how far depends on the rest of the section.
